Costa Vela is an original 3D open-world crime game that runs offline in a desktop browser. Drive or walk through a sunlit coastal city of four districts, steal cars, take eight story jobs, outrun a five-star CVPD response with roadblocks and Air One, and spend your cash on garage upgrades.

## What's inside

- Four districts: Palm Boulevard, Vela Downtown, Costa Harbour and the Neon Strip, with palms, lanes, sidewalks, traffic, pedestrians and a sunset sky.
- Twelve vehicle models, thirteen traffic cars, thirty reacting pedestrians, and a third-person camera with drag or arrow-key orbit.
- Eight story jobs with named crew dialogue, plus a repeatable strip race with a cash prize.
- Five wanted stars: cruisers, a RAM-9 interceptor, roadblocks, Air One's moving spotlight and a tactical van, all named on the dispatch board.
- Six weapon slots, a weapon wheel, muzzle flashes, hit effects and burning wrecks.
- Respect ranks, cash, Vela Motors upgrades, local saves, radio audio and a dense HUD with a live minimap.

## Controls

WASD drive or walk · Space handbrake or nitro · E enter or exit a car · click fire · 1–6 weapons · Q weapon wheel · C crew ability · G garage · R street race · B save · arrow keys orbit the camera.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.
