Costa Vela is an original 3D open-world crime game that runs offline in a desktop browser. Walk or drive through a sunlit coastal city of four districts, steal cars, work through eight story jobs with named crew, outrun a five-star CVPD response, and spend your earnings on upgrades at Costa Vela Motor Works.

Every piece of this entry was put together from scratch for the bounty, down to the street names and the garage signage.

## What's inside

- Four districts — Palm Boulevard, Vela Downtown, Costa Harbour and the Neon Strip — with palms, lane-marked roads, sidewalks, streetlights, parked cars, water and a sunset sky with fog.
- Walking with reacting pedestrians, traffic cars on road loops, twelve distinct vehicle models, and a third-person chase camera with drag or arrow-key orbit.
- Eight missions with dialogue subtitles, a checkpoint street race, and an objective panel that tracks every stage.
- Wanted levels 1–5: patrol cars, a PIT interceptor, roadblocks, Skywatch with a moving spotlight, and a tactical unit, all named with distance and status on the dispatch board.
- Six weapon slots, a weapon wheel, tracers, impact bursts and burning wrecks.
- Respect ranks, cash, three-level tuning, local saves, a start screen, procedural WebAudio, and a readable HUD with a canvas minimap.

## Controls

WASD drive or walk · Space handbrake or nitro · E enter or exit a car · click fire · 1–6 weapons · Q weapon wheel · C cycle crew ability · G garage · R street race · B save · arrow keys orbit the camera.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Add `?shot=1`, `?shot=2`, `?shot=3` or `?shot=4` to replay the four screenshot setups. No network access is needed at runtime: three.js is vendored under `game/vendor/`.
